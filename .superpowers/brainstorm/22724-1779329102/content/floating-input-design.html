All options share these behaviors
  • Auto-focus: Text field is immediately focused when the bubble appears — no extra click needed
  • Dismiss on outside click: Global mouse-down monitor detects clicks outside the panel bounds and hides it
  • Esc to dismiss: Pressing Escape hides the panel, preserving the draft
  • Draft persistence: Typing, clicking away, and re-triggering leaves the previous text intact
  • Mouse following: Panel springs toward cursor at 25 Hz when not actively typing
  • Send on Enter: Submits and routes through intent classifier
